Energy, Ecosystems, and Ecology

Energy

Life runs on energy. We know two things about energy, referred to as the Laws of Thermodynamics. The first Law is Conservation: Energy cannot be created or destroyed. However, energy can be transferred from one molecule to another (i.e. in chemical reactions) or changed from one form to another. The energy in living things is usually contained in molecules; this is called chemical energy. What are some other forms of energy? ______________________________________________________

The second Law is Entropy: In any energy conversion or transfer, some energy is lost to the system and is no longer usable. For example, cars convert the chemical energy contained in gasoline into kinetic energy to move the car. However, a lot of energy is lost (becomes unusable) in the form of heat. Living things also transfer, use, and lose energy according to the Laws of Thermodynamics. Organisms break down food molecules (releasing energy to do work), and use energy to build their structures (storing chemical energy in molecules), all the while losing energy in the form of heat.
