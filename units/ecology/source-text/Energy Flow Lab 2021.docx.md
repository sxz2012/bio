Energy Flow Lab

Introduction: You have noticed that when you exercise, you feel warmer. Lab question: How does movement affect the heat generated in a person’s body?

Experimental Roles: 2 “wet” volunteers (Warning: your hand will get wet and cold!), 2 “dry” recorders (1 person to hold/stir thermometer and take temperature readings, and 1 person to record temperatures at appropriate times).

Procedure:

Pour the water from a chilled 500-ml water bottle into each of the containers.

Turn on the thermometers, set to “Celsius”, and place one in each container.

“Wet” volunteers rub their hands together vigorously for at least 30 seconds.

When instructed, volunteers each place a hand in a container of water (up to wrist).

Volunteers should maintain their hands still or moving for the entire 5 minutes.

Recorders measure the temperature once each minute for 5 minutes and record data.

Clean up: Using a funnel, pour the water back into the water bottles over a sink. Top off water bottles as needed. Wipe any spilled water off of table and return lab baskets.
