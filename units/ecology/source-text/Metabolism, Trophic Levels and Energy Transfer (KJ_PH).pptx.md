## Slide 5

Energy and Life + Metabolism
Metabolism = chemical reactions that build up or break down molecules in living things. 

Examples:

Photosynthesis:     
6CO2 + 6H20 + sunlight 🡪 C6H12O6 + 6O2 
Respiration:	       
 C6H12O6 + 6O2 🡪 6CO2 + 6H2O    + Heat + ATP

## Slide 6

Energy and Life + Metabolism
Metabolism is a characteristic of life
Living things use energy to build simple molecules into larger, more complex ones.
Organisms can also break down molecules to release stored energy or to process as waste.
These chemical reactions are part of metabolism
Some of these larger molecules are used for structure; some have their own functions, others are used for energy storage

## Slide 19

TROPHIC LEVELS
A trophic level is any class of organisms that occupy the same position in a food chain.

Some example of trophic levels are: primary consumers, secondary consumers, and tertiary consumers. 

Each step in this transfer of 
   energy is known as a trophic level

## Slide 20

TROPHIC LEVELS
Producers (autotrophs) – produce the energy for all other organisms
Primary Consumers – eat producers
Secondary Consumers – eat the primary consumers
Tertiary Consumers – eat the secondary consumers
Decomposers – bacteria and fungi that break down dead organisms and recycle the material (not the energy!) back into the environment

Photosynthesis and Cellular Respiration
Energy passes through an ecosystem in a food chain from one trophic level to another
Producer
Converts light energy into chemical energy through photosynthesis
Energy is stored in the chemical bonds of molecules
Consumers
Convert the food they eat into energy (ATP) through cellular respiration. 

 ** ALL living things do cellular respiration **
