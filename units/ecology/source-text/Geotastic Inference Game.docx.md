When playing the geotastic game, there are two ways to explore your surroundings. You can swivel your view (look around from a fixed location). You do this by clicking and holding the button while moving the mouse), or you can advance your view. You advance your view (move forward in a certain direction) by clicking in the direction on the map you want to move (you can’t always move in all directions). When completing the 5 trials, you are allowed unlimited swivels for each round but only limited advances. See chart to the right to clarify. Do not use google to search up location clues!
