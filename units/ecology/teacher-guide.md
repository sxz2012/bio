# Ecology teacher guide reference

Source: [Ecology Unit Guide (KJ).docx](../../Ecology%20Unit%20Guide%20%28KJ%29.docx). The original file is unchanged.

This reference preserves the teacher’s learning targets, vocabulary, classwork titles, and study questions. Repeated merged table cells are included only once. Teacher study directions below are course material. No answers or supplemental biology explanations have been added.

The listed classwork has not yet been supplied. Exam date and exam scope are not specified in this guide.

### Study questions and teacher notes

- What are the criteria to determine if something is alive?
- Describe and give examples of metabolism.
- How do feedback mechanisms help to maintain homeostasis?
- Metabolism: All of the chemical reactions that build up and break down molecules in living things (e.g. photosynthesis, cellular respiration, protein synthesis, etc.).
- Homeostasis: Organisms use feedback mechanisms to maintain internal conditions within certain limits, even as external conditions change.

### Study questions and teacher notes

- Describe the movement of energy, oxygen, carbon dioxide, and heat through the organisms in a food web and their environment.
- How does energy flow relate to homeostasis and metabolism?
- What is an ecosystem?
- Explain: “Energy flows and matter cycles through ecosystems.”
- What roles do organisms play in a food web?
- What would be the impact if producers/decomposers/top predators were removed from an ecosystem?
- Give two reasons why top predators are especially at risk. (Hint: connect to ecological pyramids)
- Tip: Do NOT use these words in your writing: it, something, thing, normal, natural, balance (Be careful using: adapt, healthy)

### Study questions and teacher notes

- How have humans altered the carbon cycle?
- Describe the greenhouse effect.
- What are the major greenhouse gases and their sources?
- How does deforestation contribute to global warming (two ways!)

- How does global warming cause climate change?
- How does climate change affect biodiversity?
- How does climate change affect California?
- Describe the process of ocean acidification and the effects that it has on marine organisms (and humans).
- How does attribution science help to explain extreme weather events?
- Describe positive and negative feedback mechanisms that affect climate change.
