The collection contains 26 source files: 14 Word documents, eight slide decks (111 slides), and four PDFs. The two Ecology 2 framework PDFs are byte-identical. Text extraction supports indexing; it does not preserve all images or slide layout. Links and video activities mentioned inside documents are source content, not additional completed tasks.
