# Shawn’s High School Biology Study Collection

This project collects and reorganizes Shawn’s biology class materials to help him understand the course, review what he has learned, and prepare for quizzes and exams.

## What belongs here

- Class notes, teacher slides, handouts, and assigned readings.
- Homework, lab materials, and teacher feedback.
- Study guides, key vocabulary, diagrams, and practice questions derived from those materials.
- Exam scope and dates when available, plus topics Shawn needs to revisit.

## Organization

Organize materials by the units used in Shawn’s class. Within each unit, keep original source materials separate from study resources:

```text
units/<unit-name>/
  sources/         Original notes, slides, handouts, and assignments
  study-guide.md   Concepts, vocabulary, processes, and diagrams to review
  practice.md      Retrieval questions and application problems
  answers.md       Answers with explanations and source references
  review-notes.md  Misconceptions, missed questions, and topics to revisit
exams/
  <exam-name>/     Confirmed scope, review checklist, and targeted practice
```

Create unit and exam folders as actual materials arrive; the course syllabus should determine their names and order.

## Working principles

- Prioritize Shawn’s teacher’s materials and the stated exam scope.
- Preserve originals and record the source of reorganized content.
- Prefer suitable openly licensed illustrations from Wikimedia Commons and other open educational sources; include creator and license credits.
- Label supplemental explanations and flag unclear or conflicting information.
- Use clear language appropriate for high school biology while retaining scientific vocabulary.
- Build review around explaining concepts, recalling information, interpreting diagrams, and applying ideas.
- Keep practice answers separate so Shawn can attempt questions before checking them.
- Use missed questions and teacher feedback to guide subsequent review.
